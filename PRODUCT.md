# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary: hackathon judges seeing the site on a laptop or projector, deciding whether the idea and the model are real.
End users: Pakistani agricultural departments and research bodies such as NARC, and their field officers, often on a phone, who need to know whether a crop is ready and healthy.

## Product Purpose

Fasl turns one crop photo into a clear report: crop type, growth stage, harvest readiness, health, and what to do next. The site has to make that promise memorable, then prove it with real model metrics.

## Brand Personality

Bold, proud, Pakistani. Big type, a strong Urdu presence, and pride in what was built here.

## Anti-references

`CLAUDE.md` and `docs/` are the source of truth for theme, palette and type. Stay within them. In particular: no emojis, no made-up statistics, and outside the hero, wheat never hides content.

## Design Principles

1. The spec decides. Fonts, colours and copy come from `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/`.
2. Earn the reveal. The wheat field is the memorable moment, and acting never requires finding the hidden text.
3. Prove, don't claim. Every number comes from a real source or the real training run.
4. Urdu is part of the identity, not a translation added afterwards.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Body text meets 4.5:1 contrast. `wheat` and `wheat-deep` are never text on white. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables sway, reveal and scroll animation and shows all text plainly. Every section works at 375px. Semantic HTML throughout, with `aria-label` on icon-only buttons.
