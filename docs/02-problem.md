# 02 — The Problem

## Goal

Explain clearly what problem Fasl solves for Pakistani agricultural departments such as NARC. Leads directly into the demo.

## Copy

Section label: `The problem`

Heading: "Field teams guess. Crops pay the price."

Intro: "Agricultural departments across Pakistan still judge harvest readiness and crop health by eye and experience alone, and one wrong call can cost a whole region's season."

### Problems

1. **Harvesting at the wrong time**
   Cut too early and the grain is shrivelled and light. Cut too late and grain falls from the ear, plants lodge, or a rain spell ruins the standing crop.

2. **Disease spotted too late**
   Diseases like wheat rust spread fast across a field. By the time the damage is obvious to the eye, much of the yield is already lost.

3. **No expert nearby**
   Departments have too few agriculture officers and extension workers to cover every field. Advice that's needed today often takes days to reach the field.

4. **Unsure crop quality at the mandi**
   Without a clear picture of crop condition across fields, departments can't forecast yield, plan harvest support, or advise on quality before produce reaches the mandi.

Closing line: "Fasl turns one photo into a clear answer." with a link/button "See the demo" to `#demo`.
